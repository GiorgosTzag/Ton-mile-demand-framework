Two levers, two mechanisms

+5.5%
Red Sea rerouting · distance
Full diversion of Suez-transiting lanes via the Cape. Same cargo, longer voyage — more ton-miles. (Lever is continuous: model any share of vessels diverting.)
-21.8%
Net Gulf export loss · volume
Lose 50% of Gulf-origin cargo. Demand falls — the 2026 rate spike came from supply disruption & risk premia, not this channel.

Method: ton-miles = cargo (tonnes/yr) × one-way laden distance (nm). Red Sea rerouting affects Suez-transiting (Europe-bound) lanes only, continuously (0–100% of exposed vessels); net Gulf export loss affects all Gulf-origin lanes. Selected major lanes (~72% of Hormuz oil flows), 2025 baseline — volumes illustrative (Energy Institute / IEA / EIA order of magnitude); distances approximate.
